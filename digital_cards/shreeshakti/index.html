About Us

Our story, our passion

Welcome to Shree Shakti Kothi Ice Cream, where tradition meets taste. We specialize in authentic Kothi Ice Cream — a traditional style of natural ice cream made using fresh milk, real fruits, and premium dry fruits.


Our ice creams are made without artificial flavors or preservatives, giving you a pure and rich taste in every bite. From classic flavors to unique seasonal creations, we bring you the true essence of natural ice cream.


Located in Amreli, Gujarat, we are proud to serve happiness in every scoop to our beloved customers for years. 🍦

🏺 What is Kothi Ice Cream?

Kothi Ice Cream is a traditional Indian method of preparing ice cream using a special container (Kothi), slow churning, and natural cooling techniques. This age-old process sets our ice cream apart from machine-made varieties.
